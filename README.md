# The-Roasted-bean-PowerBI
This project analyzes coffee sales data to identify sales trends, customer purchasing patterns, top-performing coffee products, and revenue performance.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is a **coffee sales analysis dashboard created for a friend's coffee shop on a pro bono basis**.

The purpose of the project was to turn the coffee shop's sales data into clear, interactive insights that could help the business understand its sales performance, identify popular products, and recognize patterns in customer purchasing behavior.

I used **Microsoft Power BI** to clean, analyze, and visualize the data and create an interactive dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective was to answer questions such as:

* Which coffee products generate the most sales?
* Which products are the most popular?
* What times of the day have the highest sales?
* Which days generate the most revenue?
* How do sales change across different months?
* Which payment methods are used most frequently?
* Which coffee types contribute the most to overall performance?

---

## 📊 Key Analysis

The dashboard analyzes:

* **Total Sales**
* **Number of Orders**
* **Coffee Type Performance**
* **Sales by Hour**
* **Sales by Day**
* **Sales by Month**
* **Payment Method**
* **Product Performance**
* **Time-of-Day Purchasing Patterns**

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

* **Microsoft Power BI**
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** – Measures and calculations
* **Data Visualization** – Charts, cards, slicers, and interactive dashboards

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The Power BI dashboard includes interactive visuals that allow users to explore sales performance across different dimensions.

Users can filter the analysis by factors such as:

* Coffee type
* Date
* Month
* Day
* Hour
* Payment method

This makes it easier to identify sales patterns and compare product performance.

---

## 💡 Business Value

The analysis provides the coffee shop with a clearer view of its sales data and helps identify:

* High-performing products
* Peak sales periods
* Customer purchasing patterns
* Changes in sales over time
* Areas that may require further attention

These insights can support decisions around **product offerings, staffing, promotions, and sales planning**.

---

## 🤝 Project Context

This project was completed **pro bono for a friend's coffee shop** as an opportunity to apply data analytics skills to a real-world business scenario.

It allowed me to work with business data beyond a purely academic or practice dataset and focus on turning raw sales information into practical business insights.
